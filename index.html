📋 Good to Know

Classroom Basics

Straight from Ms. Fernandes — the day-to-day details families ask about most.

Dress Code

Collared or BCE shirts; bottoms 4" or less above the knee; closed-toe, closed-back shoes (no crocs, heels, UGG slippers, or boots); badge worn daily with no add-ons.

Snack & Water

Water only in the classroom. Snack should be just a little something — small, dry, and healthy.

Birthday Treats

Purchasing a treat from the cafeteria is highly encouraged (order via the district website) — please notify the teacher before sending anything in. Goody bags & invitations can't be passed out unless they're for the whole class or all the boys/girls.

Nightly Homework

20 minutes of reading, math spiral review, multiplication & division fact practice, and spelling words — checked in the take-home folder & planner daily.
